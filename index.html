How the digital SAT is scored

Each section is 54 (Reading & Writing) or 44 (Math) questions across two modules. Your raw score — questions right, with nothing subtracted for wrong answers — becomes a 200–800 section score, and the two sections add up to the 400–1600 total.

Why Module 1 matters more

The test is section-adaptive. Roughly 18 of 27 in Reading & Writing and 15 of 22 in Math send you to the harder second module, where 800 is reachable. Below that you get the easier one, and the section tops out near 600 however well you do on it.

Two questions per module don't count

Of the 98 questions on the test, 8 are pretest items College Board is trialling for future forms — 2 in every module, which leaves 50 of the 54 Reading & Writing questions and 40 of the 44 Math ones actually scored.

How close is this to the real thing?

Close enough to plan with, not a prediction. Expect ±30–70 points per section, and more in the lower half of the scale.
